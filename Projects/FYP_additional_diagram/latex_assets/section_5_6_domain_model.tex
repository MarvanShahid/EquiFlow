Core entities: Intersection, Approach/Lane, SignalPhase, Vehicle, EmergencyVehicle,
DetectionEvent, Track, PreemptionEvent, ControllerDecision, SimulationScenario, and
EvaluationRun. A DetectionEvent references a vehicle hypothesis, timestamp, confidence,
and image coordinates. A PreemptionEvent records the affected intersection/approach,
start and end times, and phases interrupted. An EvaluationRun records configuration,
seed, policy version, model version, and output metrics. Figure~\ref{fig:domain-model}
shows the entities, their main attributes, and their relationships.
